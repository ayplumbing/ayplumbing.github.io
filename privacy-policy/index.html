Water Filtration Systems for Your Home

We can help you choose and install a water filtration system suited to your home. Whether you need a localized reverse osmosis system for a tap on your kitchen counter or in your fridge, or whether you want a solution that works for your whole home, we've got you covered. We can supply filters for your home or install a filtration system you already own. We can also replace filters as part of routine maintenance. Reverse Osmosis Filters

Reverse osmosis (RO) pushes water through a semi-permeable membrane to reduce many dissolved substances. The results depend on the system and its certified performance. A typical under-sink system may include these stages:

  1. Sediment prefilter: reduces particles such as rust and sand.
  2. Carbon prefilter: can reduce chlorine, taste, and odour.
  3. Additional carbon filtration: performance depends on the filter model and its certification.
  4. RO membrane: reduces dissolved substances listed by the manufacturer.
  5. Postfilter: can improve taste before water reaches the tap.
  6. Optional remineralization: adds minerals back to the water for taste.

Whole House / Point of Entry Systems

Whole house water filters, otherwise known as Point-of-Entry (POE) systems are designed to filter the water in your entire home by treating every drop of water as it enters. Systems can combine multiple stages, such as softening to reduce hardness and filtration to reduce chlorine, taste, or odour. The right combination depends on your water and the system.

Water hardness is mainly caused by calcium and magnesium. A water softener can reduce these minerals through ion exchange. Activated carbon can reduce chlorine, taste, or odour when the selected system is designed and certified to do so. Check each product’s specifications for the contaminants it addresses.
